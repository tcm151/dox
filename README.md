# ClassicForum

Own your community. Host it yourself. Shape it how you want.

ClassicForum is a self-hosted, modern, and versatile forum platform for people who want an alternative to closed platforms like Discord; heavily centralized networks like Reddit; and aging forum software stacks. It is designed for public communities, hobby interests, community engagement, support portals or creators who want control over how their space looks, works, and grows. ClassicForum is a free and open source option that anyone can download and try out for themselves with no strings attached.

The internet works best when communities can be independently operatred, publicly accessible, and not locked behind someone else’s platform decisions. ClassicForum exists to make that path easier.

## Why ClassicForum?

- **Ownership**: run it on your own hardware or cloud server.
- **Moderation**: moderate and manage your community how you want.
- **Customization**: configure behavior, features, and branding for your needs.
- **Versatility**: great for discussion boards, personal blogs, video game devlogs, or old school classic forums.

## What You Get

- **Discoverablity** for posts, links, threads, topics, images, and search; with filters for new, hot, or top ranking content.
- **Threaded Discussions** with nested replies, direct quoting, inline editing, and threaded chains of conversation.
- **Image & Media** uploads and attach them to other content; with ongoing development to support audio and video.
- **User Engagement** to follow other users, vote on their content submissions, and get notifications for interactions.
- **Tooling** includes a suite of reporting, moderation, and content filtering tools to maintain a healthy community.
- **Roles** to give users configurable permissions, and allow them to help you manage the community.
- **Self-Hosted** and powered by Nuxt and SurrealDB, with support for embedded or remote database deployments.

# Deployment Types

## Embedded

- **It is**: everything contained in one simple package. 
- **Best for**: single instance and self-hosted on a local machine or VPS.
- **Storage**: database is stored locally, including images and media.

## Remote

- **It is**: a more advanced setup for larger communities.
- **Best for**: dedicated database hosts, shared infrastructure, or multi-instance deployments.
- **Storage**: database is remote and separate, images and media are stored locally.
